    Music bed

    Build the episode

    Each shot becomes a 1920×1080, 16 fps clip with its dialogue. Still shots get a slow push-in, and anything not ready yet is black. The output folder also holds every shot as a separate file, a timeline.edl for DaVinci Resolve and the shot list.

    Timed music and sound effects

    Add audio at absolute episode times (seconds, MM:SS or HH:MM:SS). Cues overlap freely; dialogue is preserved. Cues can follow a shot or stay at an absolute episode time.

    Change the soundtrack after rendering

    Choose an episode, preview it, then export a new soundtrack. This copies the existing picture without rendering shots or using ComfyUI. New episode builds retain a dialogue-only master so repeated mixes do not stack.

    Soundtrack exports include all enabled cues plus the whole-episode music bed above if its checkbox is selected. Older exports without a dialogue-only master retain their embedded soundtrack.
